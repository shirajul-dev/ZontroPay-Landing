Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ZontroPay collects, uses, stores, and protects data when you access the platform, submit inquiries, or operate payment workflows through our services.

1. Information we collect

We may collect account details, business contact data, platform usage metrics, and operational payment data required to provide product features and support services.

2. How data is used

Data is used to deliver platform functionality, improve product reliability, troubleshoot incidents, support onboarding, and communicate relevant service updates.

3. Data security

ZontroPay applies technical and organizational measures to protect sensitive information from unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, and accidental loss.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell customer data. Information may be shared only with trusted service partners when required for infrastructure operations, legal compliance, or customer support.

5. Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as long as needed for business and legal requirements. Users can request clarification on retention periods or account-related data handling.

6. Contact for privacy requests

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@zontropay.app or reach out through the Contact page.
